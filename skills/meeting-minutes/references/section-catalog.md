# Minutes section catalog

| # | Section | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Executive summary | 3–8 lines: purpose, conclusions and what changes after the meeting |
| 2 | Context and background | Why it was called, prior situation, problem or opportunity, references mentioned |
| 3 | Participants and roles | Who attended, apparent role, who led, absentees mentioned |
| 4 | Topics covered | Rebuilt agenda: each topic with its conclusion in 1–3 lines |
| 5 | Decisions made | Decision, who decided, scope, discarded alternatives and why, evidence |
| 6 | Agreements | Commitments and agreements between parties (not necessarily tasks), evidence |
| 7 | Tasks / action items | Task, owner, date, priority, dependency, evidence (generates `tasks.json`) |
| 8 | Open questions and pending items | What was left unresolved, who has to answer |
| 9 | Risks, blockers and dependencies | Risk/blocker, impact, mitigation mentioned, owner |
| 10 | Topics under discussion / disagreements | Different positions without closure, who holds each one |
| 11 | Key data and figures | Dates, amounts, volumes, metrics, KPIs mentioned |
| 12 | Next steps and next meeting | What comes next, milestones, date/topic of the next meeting |
| 13 | Glossary | Acronyms and internal terms used |
| 14 | Relevant quotes | Important literal sentences (commitments, definitions) |
| 15 | Follow-up message | Draft email/Teams message to send to the participants |
| 16 | Questions for the next meeting | Questions worth preparing in advance |
